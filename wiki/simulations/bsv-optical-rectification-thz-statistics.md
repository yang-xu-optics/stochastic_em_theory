# BSV Optical Rectification THz Statistics

Specification for `code/src/stochastic_em_theory/optical_rectification.py`,
`code/scripts/run_or_thz_statistics.py`, and
`code/tests/test_optical_rectification.py`. This is Stage 4 of
[[simulation-roadmap]] at the level of statistics; it has no propagation.

## Observable

Single-shot THz field amplitude `E_j` at a fixed electro-optic-sampling delay,
generated by optical rectification (OR) of one bright-squeezed-vacuum (BSV)
pulse, and its statistics over shots:

- mean field, relative fluctuation, skewness, excess kurtosis;
- mean THz energy relative to a coherent pump of the same mean photon number;
- THz energy fluctuation ratio `<U^2> / <U>^2`;
- probability density of the normalized amplitude `W = E / <E>`;
- THz power spectrum split into classical-noise terms and the spontaneous
  term.

## Field Representation

- Pump: quantized, in independent squeezed-vacuum Schmidt modes `A_k` with
  mean photon numbers `nbar_k`. Photon-number statistics are exact (number
  basis, generating function). No phase-space sampling is used for the exact
  results.
- Classical comparison: Gaussian amplitude-noise model, one real amplitude
  `X_k ~ N(0, nbar_k)` per mode. This is the macroscopic limit in which
  Wigner, Husimi-Q, and P distributions of the scaled amplitude coincide.
- THz field: first-order response; its normally ordered moments are ordinary
  moments of the pump photon number
  ([[../theory/quantum-optical-rectification-of-bsv]]).

## Model Equations And Units

All quantities are dimensionless. THz amplitudes are in units of the
amplitude emitted per pump photon; frequencies are in units of the rms width
`sigma` of the pump intensity spectrum.

```text
b_out(Omega) = b_in(Omega) - i zeta(Omega) N(Omega)
N(Omega) = sum_kl c_kl(Omega) A_k^dagger A_l
c_kl(Omega) = integral d omega  psi_k^*(omega) psi_l(omega + Omega)

low-frequency limit (Omega << sigma):  N -> N_tot = sum_k A_k^dagger A_k
E_j = N_tot,j                                   (per-photon units)

single squeezed mode, cumulants of the photon number:
  k1 = nbar
  k2 = 2 nbar (1 + nbar)
  k3 = 4 nbar (1 + nbar)(1 + 2 nbar)
  k4 = 8 nbar (1 + nbar)(1 + 6 nbar + 6 nbar^2)
independent modes: cumulants add.

<N^dagger(Omega) N(Omega)> =
    |sum_k c_kk nbar_k|^2                         coherent
  + sum_kl conj(c_kl) c_lk conj(m_l) m_k          phase-sensitive
  + sum_kl |c_kl|^2 nbar_l nbar_k                 bunching
  + sum_kl |c_kl|^2 nbar_l                        spontaneous
with m_k = <A_k A_k>, |m_k|^2 = nbar_k (nbar_k + 1).

one Gaussian mode:  |c(Omega)|^2 = exp[-Omega^2 / (4 sigma^2)]
```

## Stochastic Ensemble And Seeds

Exact results need no sampling. The classical-noise sampler is used only as a
cross-check and for histograms with detection noise: seed `20261114`, 200,000
shots per mode number in the run, fixed seeds in tests.

## Convergence And Validation

- Photon-number distribution summed to `n = 4000`; moments agree with the
  cumulant formulas to `1e-8` for `nbar` up to 12 (test).
- Wick decomposition of `<N^dagger N>` checked against an explicit two-mode
  Fock-space matrix calculation with a random complex overlap matrix,
  agreement `1e-7` (test).
- Sampler cumulants against the Gamma-distribution values (test).

## Expected Limiting Cases

```text
bright single mode:   <U>/<U>_coh -> 3,   <U^2>/<U>^2 -> 35/3 + 40/(9 nbar)
weak single mode:     <U>/<U>_coh -> 2,   <U^2>/<U>^2 -> 2/nbar
K equal bright modes: W ~ Gamma(shape K/2, mean 1)
                      <U>/<U>_coh = 1 + 2/K
                      skewness = 2 sqrt(2/K), excess kurtosis = 12/K
thermal single mode:  <U>/<U>_coh -> 2,   <U^2>/<U>^2 -> 6
spectrum, one mode:   |c|^2 (3 nbar^2 + nbar) + nbar
```

## Outputs

`results/runs/20261114-bsv-or-thz-statistics/`: three CSV tables, a summary
JSON, `parameters.yaml`, `manifest.yaml`, and a four-panel PNG.

## First Run

- Bright-limit values reproduced: enhancement 3 and energy ratio 35/3 for one
  mode; `1 + 2/K` for `K` modes.
- The spontaneous term crosses the classical-noise terms at
  `Omega / sigma = 2 sqrt(ln(3 nbar))`, which is 7.7 for `nbar = 1e6` and 10.7
  for `nbar = 1e12`. Below the crossing the classical-noise model is accurate
  to a relative `1 / (3 nbar |c|^2)`.
- Skewness and kurtosis estimators of the mode number are unaffected by
  additive Gaussian detection noise; the variance estimator is biased low
  (test).

## Out Of Scope

Phase matching, absorption, pump depletion and cascading, spatial modes,
realistic Schmidt-mode shapes, and the electro-optic-sampling response
function. These enter through `zeta(Omega)` and `c_kl(Omega)` and are the next
steps in [[../prl-restructuring-plan]].
