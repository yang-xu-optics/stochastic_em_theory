# Gorlach Et Al 2020 Quantum-Optical Nature Of HHG

## Bibliographic Metadata

- Authors: Alexey Gorlach et al.
- Year: 2020.
- Venue: Nature Communications.
- Raw source: [Gorlach et al. - 2020 - The quantum-optical nature of high harmonic genera.pdf](<../../raw/sources/Gorlach et al. - 2020 - The quantum-optical nature of high harmonic genera.pdf>)
- Ingest date: 2026-06-06.

## Core Result

The paper develops a quantum-optical description of HHG in which both the
driving and emitted fields can carry quantum statistics. It predicts features
outside a purely classical emitted-field picture, including photon-statistical
structure and quantum correlations among harmonics.

## Useful Equations Or Model Ingredients

- HHG should be separated into the material response, quantized radiation
  modes, and the measurement of emitted harmonic photons.
- The emitted field is not necessarily a set of independent classical
  harmonic waves; quantum-optical correlations can connect spectral components.

## Assumptions

- The source treats strong-field HHG with quantized light modes and an electron
  response model.
- The local HHG response and the quantum state of the drive are both relevant
  to the generated harmonic state.

## Limitations And Cautions

- Agreement of a stochastic input-field model with squeezed-vacuum diagnostics
  does not by itself prove equivalence for the generated HHG quantum state.
- A classical or semiclassical HHG simulation should state whether it predicts
  emitted intensity spectra only or also emitted photon statistics.

## Relevance To This Project

This source defines a boundary for the proposed classical stochastic theory.
It supports using quantum-optical HHG as the comparison target while warning
against overclaiming from input-field sampling alone.
