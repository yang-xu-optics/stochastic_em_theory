# Sharapova Et Al 2015 Schmidt Modes In Bright Squeezed Vacuum

## Bibliographic Metadata

- Authors: P. Sharapova, A. M. Perez, O. V. Tikhonova, and M. V. Chekhova.
- Year: 2015.
- Venue: Physical Review A 91, 043816.
- Raw source: [Sharapova et al. - 2015 - Schmidt modes in the angular spectrum of bright squeezed vacuum.pdf](<../../raw/sources/Sharapova et al. - 2015 - Schmidt modes in the angular spectrum of bright squeezed vacuum.pdf>)
- Ingest date: 2026-06-06.

## Core Result

The paper gives a Schmidt-mode description of macroscopic BSV generated by PDC.
It uses Bloch-Messiah reduction to express the field as independent collective
modes and shows that mode weights and correlations change with parametric gain.

## Useful Equations Or Model Ingredients

- The interaction kernel is decomposed into Schmidt modes with eigenvalues
  `lambda_mn`.
- Gain changes the effective modal weights through factors of the form:

```text
sinh^2(G sqrt(lambda_mn))
```

- Correlation observables depend on the gain-dependent effective mode number,
  not only on the low-gain Schmidt decomposition.

## Assumptions

- The PDC geometry supports a multimode angular spectrum.
- The collective Schmidt modes are the natural basis for describing BSV
  correlations.

## Limitations And Cautions

- A single-mode BSV model is justified only after specifying filtering, mode
  selection, and detection overlap.
- Increasing gain can reshape the effective mode basis and weights; a fixed
  low-gain mode count may be misleading.

## Relevance To This Project

This source is the main guardrail for multimode BSV. The simulation roadmap
should start single-mode for validation, then add temporal/spectral/spatial
mode structure only with an explicit mode model.
