# Yanagimoto Et Al 2024 Mesoscopic Ultrafast Nonlinear Optics

## Bibliographic Metadata

- Authors: Ryotatsu Yanagimoto, Edwin Ng, Marc Jankowski, Rajveer Nehra,
  Timothy P. McKenna, Tatsuhiro Onodera, Logan G. Wright, Ryan Hamerly,
  Alireza Marandi, M. M. Fejer, and Hideo Mabuchi.
- Year: 2024.
- Venue: Optica 11, 896-918.
- Title: Mesoscopic ultrafast nonlinear optics-the emergence of multimode
  quantum non-Gaussian physics.
- Raw source: [Yanagimoto 2024 raw PDF](<../../raw/sources/Yanagimoto%20et%20al.%20-%202024%20-%20Mesoscopic%20ultrafast%20nonlinear%20optics%E2%80%94The%20emergenc.pdf>)
- Ingest date: 2026-06-06.

## Core Result

The review argues that modern ultrafast nonlinear nanophotonics is approaching
a mesoscopic regime where a few hundred photons can trigger highly nonlinear
dynamics. In this regime, mean-field, Gaussian, and non-Gaussian quantum
features coexist and dynamically interact. The authors frame this as a new
frontier for nonclassical-light-driven phenomena, all-optical non-Gaussian
measurement, and sensing.

## Useful Equations Or Model Ingredients

- The paper emphasizes that knowing only the field amplitude is insufficient;
  higher moments are independent dynamical quantities:

```text
<a>, <a^2>, <a^3>, <a^\dagger^2 a^2>, ...
```

- For Gaussian states, first and second moments are enough. Non-Gaussianity
  appears when higher-order cumulants no longer vanish.
- Candidate non-Gaussian diagnostics include:

```text
Wigner negativity
non-Gaussian witnesses
mode-selective Wigner tomography
photon-number-resolving measurements
higher-order cumulants
conditional postmeasurement Wigner functions
```

## Assumptions

- The review is centered on ultrafast nonlinear photonics, not specifically
  HHG or THz generation.
- It treats mesoscopic dynamics as a hybrid regime rather than a purely
  classical or purely few-photon problem.

## Limitations And Cautions

- Several proposed opportunities are explicitly speculative.
- Non-Gaussian features can be highly mode-dependent and require phase-sensitive,
  mode-selective, high-efficiency detection.
- Multimode behavior can act as a decoherence channel if uncontrolled.

## Relevance To This Project

This source supplies the strongest high-level novelty framing: HHG or THz
generation driven by squeezed pump light could be positioned as
nonclassical-light-driven nonlinear dynamics, where the key question is whether
the generated harmonics/THz field, the residual pump, or conditional
postselected states develop non-Gaussian structure.
