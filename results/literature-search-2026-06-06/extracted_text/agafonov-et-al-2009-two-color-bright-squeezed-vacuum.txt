                                                         In a strongly pumped non-degenerate traveling-wave OPA, we produce two-color squeezed vacuum
                                                       with up to millions of photons per pulse. Our approach to registering this macroscopic quantum
                                                       state is direct detection of a large number of transverse and longitudinal modes, which is achieved
                                                       by making the detection time and area much larger than the coherence time and area, respectively.
                                                       Using this approach, we obtain a record value of twin-beam squeezing for direct detection of bright
                                                       squeezed vacuum. This makes direct detection of macroscopic squeezed vacuum a practical tool for
